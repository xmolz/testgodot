# IF I REMEMBER CORRECTLY - Master Dialogue Document

## Game Overview

"If I Remember Correctly" is a 2D point-and-click adventure game set in a near-future hospital run by HAVEMORE Inc, a mega-corporation that manages American infrastructure. The player controls a female protagonist who wakes up with complete amnesia in a hospital ward. She must recover her identity by using a MemoryBox device, which requires a TechPass she doesn't have. The game explores themes of memory, identity, corporate control, depression, and human connection through a verb-based interaction system (examine, talk to, pick up, use, give, flash).

### Characters

- **Player (Fiona)**: The protagonist. A woman who wakes from a coma with no memories. She is resourceful, witty, and manipulative when she needs to be. Her name (Fiona) is discovered through the MemoryBox.
- **AIda**: An AI nurse (Assigned Intelligence for Diagnostic Assistance). Speaks in corporate-friendly language. Metallic limbs, nurse attire. Manages the ward.
- **Sergey**: A Russian man in his mid-forties. Former HAVEMORE Defense research contractor. Had a heart attack. Kind, lonely, widowed. The player manipulates him emotionally to obtain his TechPass.
- **McBucket / Old Man (Patient #67)**: An elderly patient who screams "REEEEE" in his default state. Responds differently to three medications: Cannathink (becomes philosophical stoner), Invigirol (screams louder), Zanopram (falls asleep).
- **Dread**: A purple entity encountered in the intro. Represents depression and the desire to give up.
- **Hope**: A pink entity encountered in the intro. Represents the will to continue living.
- **Lewgend**: The game's developer. Appears in a fourth-wall-breaking end-of-demo scene.
- **Layla**: A female love interest teased in the end-of-demo scene.
- **Malcolm**: A male love interest teased in the end-of-demo scene (still using template sprite).
- **CEO Voss**: Head of HAVEMORE Inc. Appears briefly on TV during a news interview.

### Setting

A HAVEMORE Inc Multispecialty Recovery Facility in the Greendale precinct. The playable areas are a hospital room (main room) containing the player's bed, Sergey's bed, McBucket's corner, a medicine cabinet, a MemoryBox, a TV, and an exit door; and a connected bathroom with a toilet, mirror, and toilet paper dispenser.

### Items

- **Zanopram**: Sleep medication. Used on McBucket's medicine dispenser to put him to sleep.
- **Cannathink**: Makes McBucket enter a "high" philosophical state.
- **Invigirol**: Makes McBucket scream even more aggressively.
- **Hospital Toilet Paper**: Thick premium toilet paper. Used to clog the toilet (creating a distraction to lure AIda away from the medicine cabinet).
- **Rusty Key**: A collectible key item.
- **TechPass**: Sergey's HAVEMORE access card. The key item needed to unlock the MemoryBox.

## PART 1: MAIN STORY (Chronological)

The main story follows this critical path: Intro dream sequence -> Talk to AIda -> Try MemoryBox (locked) -> Talk to Sergey (learn about TechPass) -> Clog toilet (distraction) -> Steal medicine -> Drug McBucket with Zanopram -> Return to Sergey -> Obtain TechPass -> Use MemoryBox -> Fill insurance form -> Exit

## APPENDIX: CRITICAL PATH WALKTHROUGH

1. **Wake up** - Survive the intro by choosing to keep pushing through the darkness.
2. **Talk to AIda** - Learn about your situation, get the Neural Integrity Scan, receive the insurance form tablet.
3. **Try the MemoryBox** - Discover it's locked and requires a TechPass.
4. **Report back to AIda** - Learn TechPass takes 30 days to replace. Unacceptable.
5. **Talk to Sergey** - Learn about TechPass, ask to borrow his, get refused.
6. **Seduce Sergey** - Exploit his loneliness. Get interrupted by McBucket screaming.
7. **Go to bathroom** - Pick up thick toilet paper from the dispenser.
8. **Clog the toilet** - Drop toilet paper in toilet, flush to clog it.
9. **Wait for AIda** - She receives the maintenance alert and goes to the bathroom to investigate.
10. **Steal medicine** - While AIda is away, open the medicine cabinet. Take Zanopram (sleep med).
11. **Drug McBucket** - Use Zanopram on McBucket's medicine dispenser. He falls asleep.
12. **Return to Sergey** - Resume the intimate encounter without interruption.
13. **Obtain TechPass** - After the encounter, Sergey voluntarily offers his TechPass. Fake-refuse, then accept.
14. **Use MemoryBox** - Insert TechPass, put on headset, begin memory recovery.
15. **Fill insurance form** - Enter recovered details (name: Fiona).
16. **Exit** - Use the exit door (requires completed insurance form).
